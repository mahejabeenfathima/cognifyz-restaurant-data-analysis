# Restaurant Data Analysis & Business Insights

### Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

I worked on this restaurant dataset as part of my Cognifyz Data Analysis Internship, using Microsoft Excel to clean, transform, analyse, and present the data.

The analysis looks at different aspects of the restaurant industry, including cuisines, restaurant distribution, pricing, online delivery, ratings, geographic concentration, and customer engagement.

While working through the dataset, I focused not only on calculating results but also on preparing the data in a way that made the analysis more meaningful and easier to interpret.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of the analysis were to:

- Analyse restaurant and cuisine distribution
- Identify popular cuisines and restaurant patterns
- Examine restaurant pricing and price-range distribution
- Analyse online delivery availability and rating patterns
- Understand the geographic distribution of restaurants
- Study customer engagement through ratings and votes
- Transform the raw data into useful business insights

---

## Dataset

- **Original records:** 9,551 restaurant records
- **Primary tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Analysis:** Level 1 and Level 2
- **Data:** Restaurant, location, cuisine, rating, votes, price range, online delivery, and related attributes

### Cuisine Data Transformation

While working with the cuisine column, I found that several restaurants had multiple cuisines listed within the same record.

For the cuisine-level analysis, I separated these multi-cuisine entries into individual cuisine records. This made it possible to analyse individual cuisines more accurately instead of treating an entire combination as one category.

This expanded the analytical dataset to approximately **19K records**.

The expanded dataset was used specifically for cuisine-level analysis, while the original restaurant-level data was retained for analyses where each restaurant needed to remain a single record.

## Key Insights

Some of the findings from the analysis were:

- **North Indian cuisine** had the highest number of restaurant listings in the cuisine analysis.
- The **top 10 cuisines accounted for 71.21%** of the restaurant share analysed.
- **New Delhi had 5,473 restaurant listings**, making it the most concentrated city in the dataset.
- **Price Ranges 1 and 2 together accounted for 79.12%** of the restaurants.
- Only **25.66% of restaurants offered online delivery**.
- Restaurants offering online delivery had an average rating of **3.25**, compared with **2.47** for restaurants without delivery.
- The **3–4 rating range** was the most common, with 4,590 restaurants.
- Customer votes provided a different view of restaurant engagement; a high rating did not always mean the restaurant had the highest number of votes.
- **Barbeque Nation** had the highest total votes among the restaurants analysed, with **58,436 votes**.

These comparisons helped me look beyond individual numbers and understand how different restaurant characteristics were connected within the dataset.
